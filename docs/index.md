<section class="section section-dark">
  <div class="section-inner">
    <p class="eyebrow section-eyebrow">A process with visible handoffs</p>
    <h2>The project moves in clear stages</h2>
    <p class="section-intro">Discovery loops until the project has enough honest information to move forward. Later stages each have a bounded approval.</p>
    <div class="flow">
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Orient</h3><p>Determine whether this is a new site, redesign, or targeted improvement.</p></article>
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Assess</h3><p>Use existing pages and materials when they exist; do not make people recreate them.</p></article>
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Discover</h3><p>Interview, extract evidence, identify gaps, and generate the next conversation.</p></article>
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Approve DNA</h3><p>Create the canonical internal record for identity, beliefs, services, ministries, and operations.</p></article>
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Plan</h3><p>Define audiences, visitor journeys, sitemap, page jobs, and calls to action.</p></article>
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Write</h3><p>Distill approved DNA into public copy with traceable sources.</p></article>
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Design and build</h3><p>Create the visual system, layouts, integrations, and site implementation.</p></article>
      <article class="flow-step"><h3>Launch and own</h3><p>Validate the experience, assign ongoing owners, and maintain the canonical truth.</p></article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section assets-section">
  <p class="eyebrow section-eyebrow">Keep participation simple</p>
  <h2>What the human actually touches</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">The project may contain dozens of supporting files. Participants work through six understandable assets.</p>
  <ol class="asset-list">
    <li><strong>01</strong><span><b>Project Launch Sheet</b></span><span>Captures the project path, people, constraints, evidence, and next action in a live conversation.</span></li>
    <li><strong>02</strong><span><b>Existing-Site Findings Brief</b></span><span>Shows what a current site communicates clearly and where visitors cannot determine fit or act.</span></li>
    <li><strong>03</strong><span><b>Conversation Brief</b></span><span>Gives the facilitator the five to eight questions with the highest value for the next interview.</span></li>
    <li><strong>04</strong><span><b>What We Heard</b></span><span>Lets participants correct, confirm, defer, or assign unanswered information without reviewing raw transcripts.</span></li>
    <li><strong>05</strong><span><b>Milestone Review Packet</b></span><span>Requests focused approval for foundation, strategy, copy, design, or launch.</span></li>
    <li><strong>06</strong><span><b>Launch and Ownership Sheet</b></span><span>Records who owns every dynamic area and what must stay current after launch.</span></li>
  </ol>
</section>
