# Content and Design Pipeline

## DNA documents

Create canonical internal documents only from confirmed evidence and recorded decisions.

Each DNA document must be:

- atomic: one coherent subject;
- canonical: the single authoritative home;
- complete for its approved scope;
- source-mapped;
- owned and reviewable;
- clear about public and private use.

Keep unresolved items in Open Questions. Reference related DNA documents instead of duplicating them.

## Site strategy

After foundation approval, define:

- primary and secondary audiences;
- top outcomes;
- primary site-wide CTA;
- visitor questions and journeys;
- sitemap and navigation;
- page job, owner, sources, and dynamic system;
- launch and deferred scope.

Recommendations are allowed here but must be labeled and approved.

## Page briefs and copy

Create a brief before prose. Include page job, visitor state, required questions, hierarchy, DNA sources, modules, CTA, media, SEO/accessibility, and gaps.

Draft public copy only from approved DNA and decisions. Condense and clarify without creating new meaning. Provide source mapping for consequential claims and label editorial alternatives as proposals.

## Design handoff

Create `06-design/design.md` from approved strategy, copy, brand evidence, asset inventory, references, and constraints.

Cover purpose, audiences, brand personality, selected direction, references, anti-references, logo, color, typography, imagery, hierarchy, layout patterns, components, responsive behavior, accessibility, platform constraints, and open decisions.

Use real church imagery as evidence when possible. Do not invent brand personality unsupported by discovery.

## Required approvals

Record separate approvals for DNA, visitor strategy, public copy, and visual direction.
