# Adaptive Discovery

## Generate the next conversation

Read current state, coverage, sources, existing-site findings, prior transcripts, open questions, decisions, and the next participant's role.

Select 5–8 opening questions using this priority:

1. accuracy, safety, privacy, or launch blockers;
2. scope and sitemap dependencies;
3. contradictions affecting public claims;
4. visitor fit and next steps;
5. missing page-copy evidence;
6. optional depth.

For each question include:

- why it matters;
- what to listen for;
- optional probes;
- a completion test.

Do not ask participants to repeat trustworthy evidence. Ask them to verify it.

## Conduct the interview

At the beginning identify speakers, roles, approval authority, recording permission, and private subjects.

Throughout the conversation distinguish:

- present reality;
- aspiration;
- approved church language;
- personal opinion;
- public versus private information;
- known facts versus decisions still needed.

## Extract evidence

Break transcript content into atomic claims. Record claim, topic, status, public scope, source, owner, and notes.

Statuses:

- `CONFIRMED`
- `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION`
- `DECISION_REQUIRED`
- `CONTRADICTED`
- `ASPIRATIONAL`
- `PRIVATE`
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE`

Do not write public copy during extraction.

## Close the loop

After extraction:

1. update coverage;
2. identify missing, vague, conflicting, and private material;
3. choose the best action for each gap: interview, specialist answer, document request, decision, verification, deferral, or not applicable;
4. create What We Heard + Open Questions;
5. generate the next Conversation Brief only if another interview is the right action;
6. update state.

Stop discovery when launch-critical topics are confirmed, intentionally deferred, or not applicable. Do not continue merely because unused questions remain.
