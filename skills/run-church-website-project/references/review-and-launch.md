# Review, Launch, and Maintenance

## Review packets

Ask for one bounded approval at a time:

- **Foundation:** Is the DNA accurate and complete for its subject?
- **Visitor strategy:** Does the structure help intended visitors understand fit and act?
- **Copy:** Is it faithful, understandable, and safe to publish?
- **Visual direction:** Does the design feel truthful and help the content work?
- **Launch:** Is the public site ready, owned, and recoverable?

Do not treat silence or general enthusiasm as approval. Record person, scope, decision, date, and conditions.

## Staging checks

Validate separately:

1. source fidelity;
2. visitor journeys;
3. accessibility;
4. forms and integrations;
5. mobile and performance;
6. redirects, metadata, indexing, analytics, backup, and rollback;
7. ownership and maintenance.

Prioritize findings:

- `P0`: dangerous, materially misleading, privacy/security issue, or essential action blocked;
- `P1`: core visitor cannot determine fit or complete the primary next step;
- `P2`: meaningful confusion, distrust, or avoidable effort;
- `P3`: polish or future improvement.

## Launch

Require recorded launch approval. Verify production forms, integrations, maps, giving, events, sermons, analytics, and redirects after publishing.

## Ownership

Complete the Launch and Ownership Sheet with primary and backup administrators, content sources, owners, review cadence, form recipients, external systems, escalation paths, and transition plan.

## Maintenance

- Weekly: events, announcements, livestreams, form delivery.
- Monthly: sermons, ministries, staff, service times, broken links.
- Quarterly: homepage priorities, navigation, accessibility, analytics, access and backups.
- Annually: DNA, leadership, policies, beliefs links, brand assets, and platform plan.

Update canonical DNA before changing repeated public facts.
