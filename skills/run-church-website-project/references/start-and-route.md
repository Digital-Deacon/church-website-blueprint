# Start and Route

## Goal

Turn a short conversation into a usable project state, even when the participant has prepared nothing.

## Zero-preparation setup

Do not begin by presenting a form, a block of placeholders, or a request to complete the Project Launch Sheet. Conduct the setup as a conversation and fill the project records on the participant's behalf.

Ask a small number of related questions at a time. Start with the church name, primary contact, project type, and current website. Then gather the goal, people, available evidence, constraints, private workspace location, and recording or transcription plan. Follow an answer when useful instead of mechanically reading a checklist.

`Unknown`, `not decided`, and `we do not have that yet` are valid answers. Record them in `00-admin/open-questions.md`; do not guess, stall initialization, or turn them into homework before the project can begin.

Before initializing files, confirm the church name and where the private workspace should live. After initialization, write the captured answers into the project brief and state, summarize what was heard, list the important unknowns, and recommend one concrete next action.

## Setup questions

Capture live:

- church and primary contact;
- new site, full redesign, targeted improvement, or undecided;
- current website URL, if one exists;
- reason for acting now;
- desired change;
- knowledgeable participants;
- content, theology, design, technical, and launch approvers;
- existing domain, brand, platform, timing, budget, privacy, staffing, and other constraints;
- available source materials, brand assets, and photography;
- private workspace location;
- recording and transcription approach;
- one next action.

Incomplete answers are acceptable. Mark them unknown.

## Routing

- **New/no site:** initialize discovery from the participant's knowledge.
- **Existing site:** assess the current site before the main interview.
- **Targeted improvement:** identify affected journeys and pages; keep the scope bounded.
- **Undecided:** conduct a diagnostic conversation before committing to deliverables.

## Required outputs

- `00-admin/project-brief.md`
- `00-admin/project-state.md`
- initial `00-admin/coverage-matrix.md`
- role and approval assignments
- next action

## Completion test

Church name and private workspace location are confirmed. Project path, current website, reason, primary contact, likely approvers, evidence, constraints, interview setup, and next action are known or explicitly marked unknown.
