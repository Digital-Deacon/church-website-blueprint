# Start and Route

## Goal

Turn a short conversation into a usable project state, even when the participant has prepared nothing.

## Launch conversation

Capture live:

- church and primary contact;
- new site, full redesign, targeted improvement, or undecided;
- reason for acting now;
- desired change;
- knowledgeable participants;
- content, theology, design, technical, and launch approvers;
- existing URL, domain, brand, platform, timing, and constraints;
- available evidence;
- one next action.

Incomplete answers are acceptable. Mark them unknown.

## Routing

- **New/no site:** initialize discovery from the participant's knowledge.
- **Existing site:** assess the current site before the main interview.
- **Targeted improvement:** identify affected journeys and pages; keep the scope bounded.
- **Undecided:** conduct a diagnostic conversation before committing to deliverables.

## Required outputs

- `00-admin/project-brief.md`
- `00-admin/project-state.md`
- initial `00-admin/coverage-matrix.md`
- role and approval assignments
- next action

## Completion test

Project path, reason, primary contact, likely approvers, and next action are known or explicitly marked unknown.
