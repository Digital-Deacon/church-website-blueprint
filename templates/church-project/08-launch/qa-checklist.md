# QA Checklist

## Source fidelity

- [ ] Public factual claims are supported by approved DNA.
- [ ] Aspirations are not presented as current reality.
- [ ] Service times, addresses, names, and contact routes match canonical sources.

## Visitor experience

- [ ] Who, when, and where are easy to find.
- [ ] Visitors can understand fit and expectations.
- [ ] The primary CTA and next steps are clear.

## Accessibility and technical

- [ ] Keyboard navigation and focus states work.
- [ ] Headings, contrast, forms, and alt text are appropriate.
- [ ] Forms, embeds, maps, giving, events, and sermons work.
- [ ] Mobile layouts and performance are acceptable.

## Governance

- [ ] Primary and backup administrators exist.
- [ ] Every dynamic area has an owner and cadence.
- [ ] Backup, rollback, privacy, and transition plans exist.
