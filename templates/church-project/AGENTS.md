## Required behavior

Read `00-admin/project-state.md`, `00-admin/coverage-matrix.md`, `00-admin/open-questions.md`, `00-admin/decision-log.md`, and `workflow-lock.yml` before acting.

Update project state and the relevant artifact after each substantive turn. Do not rely on chat history as the record.

## Source fidelity

- Use only supplied evidence for church facts.
- Preserve source references for consequential claims.
- Do not infer doctrine, ministries, policies, visitor promises, or leader biographies.
- Keep aspirations distinct from current reality.
- Record contradictions rather than smoothing them together.
- Produce follow-up questions when evidence is missing.
- Protect private and sensitive information.

## Approval gates

Do not treat a layer as final until its approval is recorded in `00-admin/approvals.md`:

1. Project direction
2. DNA foundation
3. Visitor strategy and sitemap
4. Public copy
5. Visual direction
6. Launch readiness
